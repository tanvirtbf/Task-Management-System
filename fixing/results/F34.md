## 1. The four fixes F34 owns

The verification block was supposed to fix nothing — but it is where the deferred and the
newly-found land, and closing them is what makes "0 silently open" true rather than aspirational.

### ISS-095 — the tag routes get their gate

Found in F28 (the guest-revocation fallout mapped onto every revoked key *except* two routes),
deferred there because "a grant matrix cannot close a gate that does not exist." F34 built the
gate: `requirePermission("task.edit")` on `POST` and `DELETE /tasks/:id/tags*` — a tag is task
metadata, so every internal role keeps the verb and the guest loses it with **zero grant changes**,
exactly as the deferral note predicted. Plus the F8 two-layer pattern: `assertTagScope` in
`TaskMembershipService` (a copy of `assertAssignScope`) proves the grant's *reach* covers the
resolved task, on add and on remove. The two `membership` role-loops that had pinned the hole as
intended behaviour flipped to internal-204 / guest-403; module **102/102**. `/watchers/self` stays
ungated on purpose — a personal subscribe is correct for a read persona.

### ISS-096 / 097 / 098 — the F31 findings, fixed and self-proving

All three were filed in F31 with a `test.fail` annotation carrying the ISS number. F34 fixed each
and **removed the annotation** — the same e2e test that documented the finding now proves the fix:

- **ISS-096 (⌘K unbound):** a `keydown` handler in `AppShell` (wraps every authed page) navigates
  Ctrl/⌘-K → `/search`, exempting typing surfaces (tiptap owns Ctrl-K for links). The advertised
  shortcut finally does something.
- **ISS-097 (6 px overflow at 390 px):** the root cause was the *sidebar*, not the topbar — 248 px
  expanded left a 390 px viewport a 142 px content column no topbar could fit. Fixed with a one-way
  auto-collapse ≤640 px, the search label ellipsizing instead of propping the row open, and the
  on-call chip hiding ≤480 px. Measured overflow **6 → 0**; a clip-aware DOM walk named the
  offender rather than guessing.
- **ISS-098 (list-page axe criticals):** `aria-label`s on every nameless control the node dump
  identified once rows were actually rendered (the run-to-run flicker was render timing) — the
  per-row … menu, the dnd-kit drag handle, the row checkbox, the assignee trigger, the
  group/column/swimlane toggles, the ListPage header ellipsis. axe(list) criticals **4 → 0**; the
  color-contrast *serious* rows remain, recorded in the issue as the scoped follow-up they always
  were.

## 2. The symmetric proof — the gate's centrepiece

P42 (end of the testing phase) re-verified 15 representative issues and **every one reproduced** on
the broken tree. F34's `reverify-probe.cjs` replays the same 15 repros against the fixed tree, with
the pass condition inverted: each must now *fail* to reproduce.

```
15 of 15 no longer reproduce, 0 still do
PASS — the symmetric proof holds: P42 proved them real, F34 proves them gone.
```

The probe is self-cleaning and ends on the X8 baseline byte-identical (tasks 46 / lists 13 /
notif 57 / forms 0 / comments 7). One trap it embodies, worth keeping: a raw-SQL session in the
probe **must** `SET time_zone='+00:00'`, or ISS-001 false-fails by exactly 6 h — that is the F3
frame rule (`frame-note.txt`), not a regression. The probe hit it, and the fix is the one-line
`SET`, which is itself a small proof that the clock work landed correctly.

Coverage of the 15: the clock (001), guest-scope + audit leak (024, 047, 053, 060), the comment
edit-window (063), SLA birth (081), password policy (083), pool exhaustion (087), counters (046),
comment notifications (064), the form-slug self-PATCH (077), foreign-space form visibility (084),
the Origin-500 (085), and orphaned notifications (073) — a deliberate spread across auth, RBAC,
data-integrity, and infrastructure.
